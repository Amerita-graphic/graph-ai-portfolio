Bridging Agriculture & Technology

I am an undergraduate student at the University of California, Davis, pursuing a Bachelor of Science in International Agriculture Development (Expected June 2026).

My passion lies in small-scale organic vegetable production and botanical science. Alongside my studies, I run Plants4You, a successful online shop specializing in propagating and selling carnivorous and drought-resistant plants. I also give back to my community by volunteering at the Knights Landing One Health Center, providing operational support and Tagalog interpretation.

I am now exploring how Artificial Intelligence can streamline research, data management, and operational workflows within the agricultural and health sectors.

About the Course

Generative AI for Work & Research by The GRAPH Courses

No-Code AI Tools for Responsible Task Automation

This 8-week live cohort course focuses on harnessing Large Language Models (LLMs) to enhance productivity in research and professional environments. It emphasizes responsible generation of text for papers, creating structured documents, building simple research websites, and utilizing AI-automated data analysis.
